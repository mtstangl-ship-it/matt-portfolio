The hub (/case-studies) redirects immediately to /case-studies/centaur-practice (or default first case). The copy below is used by:
1. The page header shown on each individual case page (above the picker)
2. The case picker button labels

This file does NOT contain individual case study body content; that lives in COPY-case-[slug].md files. Each case study has its own meta strip customized to its subject matter.

---

## PAGE METADATA
- Page: Case Studies hub
- Purpose: Give visitors a clear, scannable index of every case study with enough preview content to choose which to read first, and signal that the case studies show process and decisions, not just outcomes (which the Impact dashboard already does)
- Primary audience: VPs of Product, Heads of Design, senior recruiters, and design directors who clicked through from Impact wanting to see how the work got built
- Desired state after visit: Visitor selects at least one case study to read in full, with confidence about which one matches their interest

## NAVIGATION CONTEXT
- Comes from: Impact page handoff CTA, Home page case study teaser cards, nav bar
- Links to: Five individual case studies (Centaur Practice, Synthetic Users, Autodesk, Wipro, EY)
- Must feel distinct from: The Impact dashboard. Where Impact shows what shipped in numbers, the Case Studies hub previews the process, decisions, and pivots behind each engagement.
