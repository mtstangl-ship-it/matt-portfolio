## PAGE METADATA
- Page: About
- Purpose: Establish who I am as a design leader — beliefs, working approach, career arc, and how I show up — so a hiring manager finishes the page with a clear mental model of how I'd operate inside their org, not just a summary of past work
- Primary audience: Senior hiring managers, design directors, VPs of Product, Heads of Design, and senior recruiters who are deeper in the evaluation process — they've already seen Impact and at least one case study and are now asking "what's it like to work with this person?"
- Desired state after visit: Visitor leaves with a felt sense of my judgment and working style — not just facts about my career — and either books a conversation or shares the page with a peer evaluator

## NAVIGATION CONTEXT
- Comes from: Hero nav bar (typical second-click from Home for senior reviewers), recruiter sharing, peer-evaluator referrals, sometimes after Impact or a case study
- Links to: Contact (primary), Impact and Case Studies as supporting links
- Must feel distinct from: Generic "About Me" pages that read as résumés in prose. This page must show how I think and decide — not just where I've worked. The motorcycle metaphor (Alice and Ayda — my BMW airheads) is the editorial through-line. The Double Diamond is the structural anchor mid-page.
