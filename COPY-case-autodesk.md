## Lesson / What I'd Do Differently

**Headline** (implied from content)
```
What I'd do differently
```

**Body**
```
The work that mattered most wasn't the five phases or the three tradeoffs. It was the discipline of forcing the operating model — people, process, platform — to carry the same logic the blueprint drew.

Next time I'd do the capacity audit before the design, not after. The tradeoffs chapter existed in my head from week one. Writing them down as a constraint document on day two would have saved four weeks of alignment rework.
```
