These assets are the source materials for the About page. Claude Design will reference them Tuesday and apply halftone/duotone treatment per the visual direction in DESIGN-NOTES.md.

## /photos-original/
Existing photos of Alice (1978 BMW R100/7) and Ayda (1977 BMW R75/7) in various contexts — Colorado motel, abandoned asylum, DC street, helmet reflection, road shadow shots.

## /diagrams-factory/
BMW airhead factory fiche diagrams (royalty-free historical artifacts):
- Gauge cluster exploded view (00008271)
- Tafel 3 engine assembly diagram
- Boxer engine cutaway illustration

## /photos-new/
New photos to be added: hands-on-work shots, garage/workshop context, close-up component details. These are the highest-leverage additions for the page — they prove the "I work on old BMWs" claim with specificity.

## Treatment direction
Per DESIGN-NOTES.md: all photos halftone or duotone treated, single accent color (teal) over dark base. Photos must NOT appear as raw photographs on the site — that breaks the visual register.

## Mixing direction
Photos and factory diagrams interleave throughout the page. Photos show the life. Diagrams show the structure. Together they create a "personal service manual" rhythm.
