SU-02 · MOBILE LOGIC

The matrix re-groups by environment, not by method. Each card is one QA environment with REAL stacked directly over SYNTHETIC — so the comparison the matrix exists to make survives the vertical collapse. The meters sit at the right edge of each row, aligned, so you still read the head-to-head at a glance.

Why not the brief's "two method blocks"? Stacking all of REAL then all of SYNTHETIC separates the two halves of every comparison by a full scroll. Per-environment keeps each duel intact. The two-block pattern is the fallback if Matt prefers method-first reading.

Margin fix. The bordered container takes max-width:100% + explicit padding, resolving the edge-overflow flagged in design-notes for boxed elements at 320–414px.

The punchline metrics stack full-width beneath the cards — 3, 1, $8K→$0 still close the section.

↘ Evidence Ladder · read & trade-offs

§ 1 · The ladder marksWhy count beats color

The brief asked for four visually distinguishable confidence levels without color-only encoding. A 4-segment meter where filled count is the level solves it cleanly: Ship reads as four, High as three, Mid as two, Baseline as one — verifiable in grayscale, by a screen reader (segment count), and at a glance. Mid additionally drops from accent to a neutral fill, giving it redundant encoding (count and weight) so the "directional, not decision-ready" status reads even faster. This is the single most portable decision in the artifact.

§ 2 · Comparison, not gridStructural contrast

The two methods share columns and meter positions, so the comparison is spatial: drop your eye down any environment and the two meters line up for direct height comparison. The verdict strip names the outcome per column — three replaces, one augments — which sets up the footer metrics without restating them. UAT is the honest one: synthetic augments rather than replaces, and the artifact says so in the open.

§ 3 · Risks for the port

  • Meter legibility at density. 9px segments are fine on desktop; on the mobile cards they shrink to ~5px — keep the four-segment count readable, don't let them blur into one bar.
  • Verdict glyphs. ▣ / △ carry meaning alongside the words; if Matt wants pure type, drop the glyphs but keep "Replaces / Augments."
  • Synthetic-row tint. The faint accent wash on the synthetic row aids scanning but is close to the verdict accent — verify it doesn't muddy at final accent color.
