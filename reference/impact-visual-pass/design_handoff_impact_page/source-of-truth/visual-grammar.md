# Visual Grammar — Fiche Vocabulary

## The aesthetic anchor
Technical illustration. Motorcycle fiche. Service bulletin. Patent drawing. The portfolio as an exploded-view schematic. Not mission control. Not HUD. Not dashboard chic. Technical illustrator.

## Core elements
- Title blocks: drawing-convention corner cards (part number, revision, sheet, scale, drawn by, date)
- Leader lines: thin, mono-width, no arrowheads, end in crosshair or tick
- Dimension rules: horizontal or vertical lines with tick marks, mono label centered
- Part numbers: every callout gets one (01-F, 01-B, 02-A format)
- Crosshair alignment marks: small (+) at registration points
- Revision callouts: REV. 02 · REDRAWN — faint red-orange typewriter stamp
- Figure stamps: FIG. 01, FIG. 02 corner of major visual elements
- Construction lines: faint dashed, showing relationships and assembly
- Sheet footer: bottom strip on every page (sheet, scale, drawn, approved, date, rev)
- Notes section: numbered drawing notes at bottom

## Typography
- Monospace: labels, part numbers, stamps, callouts, metadata
- Sans: body, headlines, subheadlines
- Weights: 400 regular, 500 medium only. No 700.

## Color
- Background: dark (#0a0f0e range)
- Accent: teal (#1dcfaa range), used sparingly — one per viewport
- Warning/revision: faint red-orange for stamps only
- Rule: color encodes meaning, never decorates

## Motion principle
"The schematic drawing itself as you read." Lines trace. Stamps appear. Dimension rules extend. Single-fire animations only. Never looping ambient motion.

## What this is NOT
- Not a Figma community dashboard
- Not a SaaS landing page
- Not mission-control / HUD aesthetic
- Not an attempt to look like Linear or Vercel
- Not decorative particles or gradients
